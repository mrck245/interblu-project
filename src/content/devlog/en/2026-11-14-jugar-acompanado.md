In the post about our 60 languages we said we were considering new game modes. That decision is made: these are all of them.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-ffa.gif -->

## Single player

The full campaign: four biomes, twelve levels, their challenges and their poems. It has save slots, so you can keep several runs going at once.

## Co-op

Two players go through the same campaign in the same level. Points, frogs and items are shared, and challenges affect both. If you're playing alone, the **CPU** can be your partner.

## Free-for-all

Two to four players in one-minute rounds, one for each level in the game. Shots hit the other players. You earn points for hitting, eliminating, picking up items and still standing when the round ends. Some rounds have their own challenge, and at the end there are awards for whoever eliminated the most, hit the most and collected the most items.

The CPU can fill the other seats, so you can play it alone too.

## Where to play

- **On the same device**: each player with their own part of the keyboard or their own controller.
- **On a local network**: several devices on the same network.
