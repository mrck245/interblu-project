Today TULIPS begins. This post is about the idea we started with.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-pradera.gif -->

## The idea

TULIPS is a top-down pixel art action game. Each level is a field full of flowers where **frogs** wake up and keep coming. Your job is to shoot your way through without wrecking the flowers.

Every level follows the same rhythm:

1. You find a **note**.
2. The frogs wake up and the **challenge** begins.
3. Once you beat it, the **portal** to the next level opens.

## Powers

Some items change your shot, like the **blade**. If you get hit, you lose the power, so keeping it is part of the game.

## Where we start

The first biome is the **meadow**: tulips, grass and three levels to learn the basics. The plan is for a run to go through several biomes, each with its own terrain and music.

We'll keep sharing our progress here.
