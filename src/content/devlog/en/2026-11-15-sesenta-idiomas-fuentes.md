Translating a game into 60 languages isn't just translating sentences. In TULIPS all the text is pixel art, and most pixel fonts only cover the Latin alphabet.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-idiomas.png -->

## A font for each script

We looked for pixel fonts for every writing system: Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai, Georgian, Armenian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and more. When we couldn't find one that matched the rest of the game, we drew the missing characters ourselves.

One example: for Vietnamese, we built the tone marks out of the main font's own letters, so all the text looks equally sharp.

Every font we use, with its author and license, is listed in the game's credits.

## The title in each language

The TULIPS logo changes too. In Arabic, Persian, Russian, Greek, Hebrew, Georgian, Bulgarian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese it has its own drawing.

If you play in your language and spot a translation mistake, please let us know: it helps a lot.
