After you read a level's note, its **challenge** begins: a rule that only lasts for that level. Each biome has six challenges of its own, and the game shuffles them across its three levels, so no two runs play the same.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-retos.gif -->

## Some examples

- **Metronome** (meadow): your speed switches between slow and fast every five seconds.
- **Fifth shot** (meadow): every fifth shot turns golden and deals ten times the damage.
- **Golden frog** (meadow): every twenty seconds a frog starts to glow. It's faster, but it always drops an item.
- **Fog** (jungle): the level goes dark and you can't trust what you see.
- **Tides** (jungle): six seconds without frogs, then six seconds of stampede.
- **Time stop** (desert): every twelve seconds all frogs freeze for a moment.
- **Thirst** (desert): you slowly lose speed, and defeating a frog refreshes you.
- **Blizzard** (snow): every few seconds a gust pushes you.
- **No loot** (snow): no frog drops items, but you move faster.

## Why challenges

Challenges make replaying a level worth it. Many of them give you an advantage and a drawback at the same time, and the trick is to use the first before the second catches up with you.
