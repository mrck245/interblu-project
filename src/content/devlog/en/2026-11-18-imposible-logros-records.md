Finishing TULIPS is just the beginning. This post is for anyone who wants to keep playing.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-records.png -->

## Impossible mode

You pick it when you create a save, and it changes several things at once:

- You have **two hearts** instead of three.
- Frogs are a tougher kind, spawn twice as fast and rest less between jumps.
- A single hit takes away your whole power, not just one level.
- Frogs don't drop hearts.

You can play it alone or in co-op.

## 55 achievements

TULIPS achievements are specific challenges you can go for on purpose, in every mode. A few examples:

- Finish a level in under 20 seconds.
- Finish all 12 levels without taking damage, even across different runs.
- Finish a biome in co-op without breaking a single flower.
- Win a free-for-all after being last at the halfway point.

## Records

Each mode has its own records page: single player, co-op, impossible solo, impossible co-op and free-for-all. Under each set of records are that mode's achievements.

## Skins

You can choose how your character looks, and in multiplayer each player picks their own.
