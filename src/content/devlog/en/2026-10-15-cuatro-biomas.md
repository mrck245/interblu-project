TULIPS is split into four biomes with three levels each. With the jungle puddles, which we just finished, every biome now has its own terrain. We wanted each one to feel different not just in its colors, but in how you move through it.

<!-- IMAGE: public/devlog/tulips-biomas.png -->

## Meadow

The starting point. Tulips, grass and the first frogs. This is where you learn the basics: shoot, protect the flowers and keep moving.

## Jungle

The jungle has **puddles**. Step into one and you fall in: you lose a heart and come back at the last safe spot you walked on. Frogs can sink into them too. The last level has more puddles than the others, and they aren't always in the same place.

## Desert

**Sand** cuts your speed in half. Moving well, where and when, matters as much as aiming.

## Snow

**Ice** is slippery: it's hard to stop and turn. Winter also has its own kinds of frogs.

## Music

Each biome has its own music, plus a second track that kicks in when the fight gets serious.

## And after that?

Four biomes are the heart of a run. There's something more, but we'd rather you find it by playing.
