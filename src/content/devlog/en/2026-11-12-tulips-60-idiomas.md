This is an update on the current state of TULIPS.

## Localization

We have added **19 new languages**. TULIPS goes from 41 to **60 available languages**. We want the game to be accessible to players from different regions from day one.

## Game modes

In addition to the Free-for-all mode, we are evaluating new game modes. We will publish the details once the decision has been made.

## Release

We estimate the release for **January 2027**. The date may change depending on development progress; any change will be announced in this devlog.
